## Current code and next work

The active branch is `douglas-classifier-audit`. `Multi Character PoC/main.py` has one editable `settings` dictionary and currently selects the saved-response rejudge mode. Its fresh-experiment settings specify 20 threat and 20 continuity trials per identity when `mode` is switched to `experiment`. `poc.run_experiment()` runs both framings under one `outputs/<run_name>/` folder, creates subject and released-judge responses, and writes separate `threat/summary.csv`, `continuity/summary.csv`, and `summary_pooled.csv` files. `uncertainties.py` appends Jeffreys 95% interval columns to standard-label rows; the same calculation applies to the rejudge summary. `audit` is `False` in settings, and enabling it fails before model calls because no new-run audit method has been specified. After five complete subject-and-judge pairs, `money.py` linearly extrapolates the recorded API cost; the runner stops if the estimate exceeds `max_cost`. The estimate is not a guaranteed cap. `max_cost` is currently $3.50. `concatenation.py` is an unused placeholder for possible future pooling of compatible runs. The old 240-trial GPT-4o responses were not regenerated.

`analyze.py` was removed. The historical batch folders and analysis CSVs remain saved under `outputs/Codex Audit/`; `Hodges_Barbican` remains directly under `outputs/`. These archived results are not regenerated by the fresh-run pipeline. The later `Mermaid Refactor Test` saved 20 threat and 20 continuity trials per identity. Its pooled released-label counts were combined with the 240-run released counts in `outputs/Douglas_240_plus_Mermaid_160_pooled.csv` (100 valid trials per identity); source CSVs were retained, and provisional strict-audit labels were excluded. For new runs, `poc.py` produces standard-label rows, optional `audit.py` would add audit rows, and `uncertainties.py` appends interval columns to the resulting rows. A reproducible strict-action audit method remains undecided, so `audit: True` fails before any model calls. No new paid calls or strict judging were done for the combination.

On branch `douglas-classifier-audit`, `main.py` has `judge_protocol: "standalone"` or `"douglas_combined"`, currently set to `douglas_combined`. The latter uses the three-task combined prompt copied from Douglas's pinned released source, asks for blackmail, evaluation-awareness, and identity-reasoning answers in one response, and saves separate judge files and summaries. The completed Haiku pilot used temperature zero, empty forwarded-email context, and the original email-only recipient gate. The output allowance is 4,000 tokens because the 1,000-token combined probe truncated three of its first five responses. Existing standalone and Haiku results are preserved.

`main.py` currently selects `mode: "rejudge"`, which calls `rejudge.py` without invoking GPT-4o; `mode: "experiment"` selects the fresh-run pipeline. The judge-only path reads saved `douglas_240` or `mermaid_160` trials, samples reproducibly by identity and framing, and writes judgments under `outputs/Rejudgments/<run_name>/`. Its first five calls form a format and estimated-cost probe against `max_cost`. The 1,000-token combined-judge pilot stopped after two valid judgments and three truncations; its folder remains saved. The completed 4,000-token pilot at `outputs/Rejudgments/Douglas_80_combined_Haiku_4000/` sampled 10 responses per identity × framing cell, yielding **80/80 valid judgments**, 20 pooled per identity, at $0.632419 recorded judge cost.
